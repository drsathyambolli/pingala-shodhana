Type or paste in Devanagari, IAST or Telugu script. End pādas or half-verses with । ॥ or a new line. Keep the sandhi as written (saṃhitā): weights are counted across word boundaries.

Chando-Sahāyaka · छन्दःसहायकः

For composers. Write words separated by spaces, one half-verse or pāda per line. The assistant tries kośa synonyms (same liṅga and ending), filler particles (च हि तु एव अपि वै खलु) and word reordering, rescans every candidate and keeps the edits that bring the verse closer to the metre.

Lakṣaṇa-Yantra · लक्षणयन्त्रम्

Paste a lakṣaṇa verse (for example from the Vṛttaratnākara). The Yantra reads the gaṇa words (म्भौ नतौ ताद्गुरू …) and the number words for yati (जलधि-षड्-अगैः = 4 · 6 · 7), scans the verse itself, and accepts the metre only when the definition and the verse agree, because a lakṣaṇa is composed in the metre it defines.

Evaluation on the bundled lakṣaṇas

Runs the Yantra on every core lakṣaṇa and compares its reading with the known gaṇa formula and yati.
